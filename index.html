Sandbox shows what your Tempest weather station is measuring right now, with a short forecast beside it. This manual explains every number on the screen in plain English. Each yellow numbered marker on a picture matches the definition with the same number just below it.


    

      
01Quick start

      

        
3 sWind updates

        
1 minFull reading

        
5 minForecast refresh

      

      
    
        
  1. Open Settings. Tap the gear in the top corner.
    2. 
        
  2. Paste your access token and tap Connect. You make the token at tempestwx.com, under Settings, then Data Authorizations. It stays on this device.
    3. 
        
  3. Watch the four tests. Each one turns to a green tick when it works. 
    4. 
        
  4. Read the screen. When the pill at the top says Live, readings are arriving.
    5. 
      

      
TipNo station handy? Add #demo to the end of the web address to try the app with invented readings. No token or connection is needed.

03The Now card

      
The top-left card: the air around your sensor and how wet it is.

      
      
    
        
  1. 1
    Place
    The name of your station, taken from your Tempest account.
    2. 
        
  2. 2
    Temperature
    The air temperature at the sensor, in °C. It updates with each full reading, about once a minute.
    3. 
        
  3. 3
    Sky summaryForecast
    A few words and an icon for the sky, for example Partly Cloudy.
    4. 
        
  4. 4
    Feels like
    What the air feels like on your skin, worked out from the sensor's own temperature, humidity and wind. The line underneath says which rule was used (table below).
    5. 
        
  5. 5
    Rain todayForecast
    Rain so far today in millimetres. 1 mm is one litre of water on every square metre of ground. Underneath: Raining now or Dry now.
    6. 
        
  6. 6
    Rain, last minute
    Rain from the most recent minute, with a word for how hard it is falling (scale below). It reads 0.00 and "No rain" when it is dry.
    7. 
        
  7. 7
    High / LowForecast
    Today's highest and lowest forecast air temperature.
    8. 
        
  8. 8
    Rain time todayForecast
    How many minutes it has rained so far today. Yesterday's minutes are underneath.
    9. 
        
  9. 9
    SunriseForecast
    Sunrise time today, with sunset underneath.
    10. 
        
  10. 10
    Reading taken at
    The time of the latest full reading from the sensor. If it stops moving, check the .
    11. 
      

      
NoteItems tagged Forecast come from Tempest's forecast service. They disappear when Unit only is on.


      
Which "Feels like" is used

      

        Wind chillAir is 10 °C or colder and the wind is above 4.8 km/h. Uses the Environment Canada formula and the last minute's average wind.
        HumidexAir is 27 °C or warmer and humidity is 40% or more. Adds the effect of humid heat.
        Same as air tempEverything in between. No adjustment is needed.
      


      
Rain words

      
The app turns the last minute's rain into a pace in millimetres per hour, then picks the word using Tempest's own categories.

      

        Very lightunder 0.25
        Light0.25 to 1
        Moderate1 to 4
        Heavy4 to 16
        Very heavy16 to 50
        Extreme50 and up
      

      
Millimetres of rain per hour. Hail is never shown, because Tempest marks its hail detection as experimental.

    


    

      
04The Wind card

      
The card on the right. The big dial is live: it moves about every 3 seconds.

      
      
    
        
  1. 1
    Wind direction
    Where the wind is coming from, in words. "From the NW" means it blows out of the north-west toward the south-east. It says Calm when the wind is under 0.3 km/h.
    2. 
        
  2. 2
    Compass ring
    N, E, S and W, with a tick every 10°. North is at the top.
    3. 
        
  3. 3
    Wind arrow
    The dot sits on the side the wind comes from, and the arrow points the way it is blowing. The dashed streaks flow the same way, faster in stronger wind. The arrow sways a little when the wind is gusty, and fades when it is calm. If live readings stop for 15 seconds, the streaks and sway freeze, so movement always means data is arriving.
    4. 
        
  4. 4
    Wind speed
    The big number: the wind right now, in km/h. It is smoothed so it glides instead of jumping. If the live stream pauses, it shows the station's one-minute average until the stream returns.
    5. 
        
  5. 5
    Speed ring
    The blue ring fills as the wind rises: about three-tenths of the way round at 10 km/h, six-tenths at 30 km/h, and all the way round at 60 km/h or more. Light winds get extra room so they are easy to see.
    6. 
        
  6. 6
    Gust marker
    The gold tick on the ring marks the gust, the strongest puff of the last minute. It shows only when the gust is at least 0.5 km/h above the current speed, so in steady wind it stays hidden.
    7. 
        
  7. 7
    Bearing
    The same direction as a number of degrees: 0° is north, 90° east, 180° south and 270° west.
    8. 
        
  8. 8
    Gust
    The strongest puff in the last minute, in km/h. A puff is the highest of the sensor's 3-second readings. Updates once a minute.
    9. 
        
  9. 9
    Lull
    The calmest moment in the last minute, in km/h. Updates once a minute.
    10. 
        
  10. 10
    5 min high
    The highest live reading in the last five minutes. Updates live.
    11. 
        
  11. 11
    Five-minute graph
    A line of live wind speed. The left edge is five minutes ago and the right edge is now. A higher line means a windier moment.
    12. 
      


      
Gust, lull and average

      
The sensor checks the wind every 3 seconds, which is 20 checks a minute. Tempest then reports three summary numbers for that minute.

      
      
    
        
  1. A
    Gust
    The highest reading. The strongest puff.
    2. 
        
  2. B
    Average
    All twenty readings added up and divided by twenty. The steady wind in between.
    3. 
        
  3. C
    Lull
    The lowest reading. The calmest moment.
    4. 
      

      
NoteGust and Lull arrive once a minute with the full reading, so a puff you watch on the dial can take up to a minute to appear in the Gust figure. The dial, the big number and the 5 min high update live.


      
Reading the direction

      
Wind is named for where it comes from. A north wind blows toward the south. The dial points the dot at the side the wind arrives from.

      
    


    

      
05The Conditions card

      
Twelve tiles under the Now card. Each shows a main number with a small line of extra detail beneath it.

      
      
    
        
  1. 1
    Humidity
    How much water vapour the air holds, as a percentage of the most it could hold at this temperature. 100% means saturated: fog, dew or rain. The small line is the dew point.
    2. 
        
  2. 2
    Pressure
    The weight of the air above you, in millibars (mb, the same as hPa). About 1013 mb is average at sea level. The arrow shows the trend: ↗ rising usually means settling weather, ↘ falling usually means change on the way. "stn" is the raw reading at the sensor.
    3. 
        
  3. 3
    UV index
    How strong the sun's burning rays are. 0 at night. The word underneath is the level (scale below).
    4. 
        
  4. 4
    Solar
    How much sunlight energy is landing on the sensor, in watts per square metre (W/m²). Roughly 1,000 W/m² is full summer sun. Underneath is brightness in lux (klux means thousands of lux).
    5. 
        
  5. 5
    Dew point
    The temperature the air must cool to before dew or fog forms. A higher number means stickier air. The word underneath is the comfort level (scale below).
    6. 
        
  6. 6
    Wet bulb
    The lowest temperature evaporating water could cool the air to. It is never higher than the air temperature. Underneath is Delta-T, the air temperature minus the wet bulb. A big gap means dry air, where water and sweat evaporate fast. A gap near zero means damp air (picture below).
    7. 
        
  7. 7
    Air density
    How heavy the air is, in kilograms per cubic metre. About 1.2 is normal near sea level. Hot or humid air is a little thinner, so the number drops. Thinner air means less drag on anything moving through it. Underneath is the raw station pressure used in the sum.
    8. 
        
  8. 8
    Rain yesterdayForecast
    Yesterday's total rain in millimetres, with the minutes it rained underneath.
    9. 
        
  9. 9
    Lightning
    Strikes detected in the last minute, and how far away they were on average. None means a quiet minute. A fresh strike shows Detected and its distance straight away, until the next minute's reading.
    10. 
        
  10. 10
    Lightning 1 hrForecast
    Strikes in the last hour. Underneath: the count for the last 3 hours and how long ago the latest strike was.
    11. 
        
  11. 11
    Sensor battery
    The sensor's battery voltage. The line underneath shows whether it is rising, falling or steady over up to 30 minutes. It says Measuring for the first 10 minutes. A rising voltage in daylight hints that the solar panel is charging it.
    12. 
        
  12. 12
    Sensor info
    The sensor's firmware version (its built-in software), its radio signal strength in dBm, and Up, the time since it last restarted. For signal, closer to zero is stronger: −55 is better than −85.
    13. 
      


      
UV index words

      

        Lowunder 3
        Moderate3 to 6
        High6 to 8
        Very high8 to 11
        Extreme11 and up
      


      
Dew point words

      

        Dryunder 5°
        Comfortable5 to 10°
        Slightly humid10 to 16°
        Humid16 to 21°
        Muggy21° and up
      


      
Wet bulb and Delta-T

      
      
NoteDew point, wet bulb, Delta-T and air density use Tempest's own figures when the forecast is on. When Unit only is on, the app works them out from the sensor's temperature, humidity and pressure.

    


    

      
06Controls and settings


      
Unit only

      

        Unit onlyOff. Sensor readings and the forecast are both shown.
        Unit onlyOn. Only what the sensor itself measures. The forecast is not requested at all.
      

      
With Unit only on, these items disappear: the sky summary, Rain today, High / Low, Rain time today, Sunrise, Rain yesterday and Lightning 1 hr. Pressure shows the sensor's raw reading instead of the sea-level figure. Your choice is remembered on this device.


      
Status pill

      

        LiveConnected, and live wind readings arrived in the last 15 seconds.
        QuietConnected, but no live wind reading has arrived for over 15 seconds. The app keeps listening and turns Live again when readings resume.
        OfflineNot connected. The app retries on its own, and reconnects when you return to it.
        DemoInvented readings, from the #demo address. No connection is made.

07Help

      
The big wind number and Gust don't match.
That is normal. The big number is the wind right now. Gust is the strongest puff of the last minute, so it is usually higher. Both are right.

      
There is no gold marker on the ring.
It appears only when the gust is at least 0.5 km/h above the current speed. In steady wind it stays hidden. It also needs the first full reading, which takes up to a minute after connecting.

      
Why do Gust and Lull change only once a minute?
They are summaries that Tempest sends with each full reading. The dial, the big number and the 5 min high update live.

      
Some tiles have disappeared.
Unit only is on. It hides everything that comes from the forecast. Turn it off at the top to bring them back.

      
Rain today shows dashes.
That total comes from the forecast service, so it shows dashes while it loads. It is hidden in Unit only.

      
Pressure differs from other weather apps.
Most apps quote sea-level pressure, which is adjusted for height. Sandbox shows that too when the forecast is on, with the sensor's raw reading as "stn". In Unit only it shows the raw reading, which is a little lower.

      
Is my token safe?
It lives only in this browser on this device and is sent only to Tempest. It is never in the page or on GitHub, and the log hides it. Tap Remove in Settings to delete it.

      
The status says Quiet or Offline.
Quiet means connected with no live wind reading for over 15 seconds. Offline means the connection dropped. The app retries by itself, waiting a little longer each time up to 30 seconds, and reconnects when you come back to it. If it stays that way, open Settings and tap Run again.

      
How do I try it without a station?
Add #demo to the end of the web address. You will see invented readings and no connection is made.
